# Orca Terms of Use

> **Draft, not final.** These terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer. They cover the friends-only test version of Orca and must be reviewed by a qualified person before Orca's public launch.

**Version 1 · Last updated:** November 4, 2026

Orca is made and run by an individual developer in Massachusetts, USA. By using Orca you agree to these terms and to the [Privacy Policy](https://benvieten.github.io/orca-legal/privacy).

## Who can use Orca

You must be **18 or older**. One account per person, and it must be yours.

## What you post

- You own what you post. You let Orca store it and show it to your friends so the app can work, and nothing else.
- Post only your own meals, workouts and numbers, and photos you have the right to share.
- Posts are final: they can't be edited or deleted one by one, so scores stay fair. Comments you write can be deleted.

## No tolerance for objectionable content or abuse

Orca has **zero tolerance** for objectionable content and abusive users. Don't post or send:

- nudity or sexual content;
- intimate images of anyone who hasn't agreed to them being shared;
- any sexual content involving someone under 18. Orca reports it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as the law requires, and keeps it only as long as the law requires;
- harassment, bullying, threats, or hate based on who someone is;
- content that's violent, illegal, or encourages self-harm or dangerous eating;
- anyone else's private information;
- spam, or fake numbers meant to cheat the scores.

**Ratings are for meals and workouts, never for people's bodies or looks.** Rate the meal or the workout, not the person.

## Reporting and blocking

Anyone who can see your profile, friends or not, can report it: your name, username and picture (for example, for impersonation or an offensive name). Your friends can also report your posts, comments and recaps. Report something from its menu, and block anyone from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and people who post it are removed from Orca.

**Automatic holds.** When several different people report the same post, comment, recap or person within a day, Orca hides it automatically. If it's a person, Orca also locks their account: they're signed out within an hour and can't sign in. Reports from accounts less than a day old don't count toward this. A person then reviews it within 24 hours and either removes it for good (for a person, a ban) or brings it back as it was. Something brought back isn't hidden automatically again.

While your account is on hold, your days still close and count toward your scores, goals and streak as usual, even though you can't post. They aren't made up after the hold ends.

To contest a hold or a ban, email **orcaapp.help@gmail.com**.

## Removing intimate images

If an intimate image of you (or of someone you're authorized to act for) is on Orca without consent, you can ask for it to be removed, whether or not you use Orca. Email **orcaapp.help@gmail.com** with:

1. your name, or the name of the person you act for, and your signature (typing your full name counts);
2. enough to find the image: whose post it is, the day it was posted, and what it shows;
3. a short statement that you believe in good faith it was shared without consent, and anything that helps show that;
4. how to contact you.

If you use Orca, you can also report the post from its menu. Orca removes the image, and makes reasonable efforts to remove any known identical copies, within **48 hours** of a valid request, and tells you what it did.

## Copyright

Post only what you have the right to share. If you believe something on Orca infringes your copyright, email **orcaapp.help@gmail.com** with: your contact details and signature; the work you own; where it is on Orca (whose post, which day); a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorized; and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you own the copyright or are authorized to act for the owner. Orca removes infringing content, and removes the accounts of people who infringe repeatedly.

## Health information

Orca isn't medical advice. Scores and goals are for motivation among friends. Talk to a professional before changing your diet, sleep or exercise.

## Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account. If your account is on hold or banned, you can't sign in to do that, so email **orcaapp.help@gmail.com** instead. Orca can suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.
